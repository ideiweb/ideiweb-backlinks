# How Much Does Custom Branded Canned Water Cost in 2026?

If you're looking to elevate your brand with sustainable, eye-catching hydration, custom branded canned water is a powerful choice. But the big question on every marketer's mind is: **how much does it actually cost in 2026?** Prices vary based on volume, design complexity, and logistics, but we've broken down the numbers so you can budget with confidence.

## Average Pricing Breakdown for 2026

In 2026, the cost per can typically ranges from **$0.50 to $1.50** for standard orders, with smaller runs (10,000 cans) on the higher end and larger volumes (100,000+ cans) dropping significantly. This price includes the can, water, labeling, and basic packaging. Premium options like full-wrap labels or custom caps add $0.10–$0.30 per can.

## Factors That Influence the Final Price

Several key factors affect your total investment. **Order quantity** is the biggest driver—massive volume discounts can cut per-unit costs by 40% or more. **Design complexity** also matters; a simple one-color logo costs less than a full-wrap photo-quality design. Additionally, **shipping distance** and **delivery timeline** can add 10–20% to your total if you need rush service.
